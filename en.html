Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Introduction

M-Cal is a calendar application provided by an independent developer. The App respects your privacy and does not collect or transmit any personal data.

Data Handling

All data used by the App is stored exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Data TypePurposeStorage
Calendar dataView, create, edit, and delete eventsOn device
Location (addresses attached to events)Display in map appsOn device
Notification settingsEvent reminder notificationsOn device
App settingsSave display and appearance preferencesOn device

Permissions

The App uses the following system permissions, all necessary for its core functionality.

  • Calendar access — Used to read and write events.
  • Notifications — Used to send event reminder notifications.

Third-Party Data Sharing

The App does not provide, sell, or share your data with any third party. No advertisements or analytics tools are used.

External Service Integration

The App can display event locations in Apple Maps or Google Maps. When opening a map app, the event address is passed to the respective map app. This only occurs through user action.

Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed at any specific age group and does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as needed. Significant changes will be communicated through app updates.
